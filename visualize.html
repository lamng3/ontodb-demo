Step through two of OntoDB's algorithms, one page read at a time.

Watch a range scan

Pick a range and step through BeginRange(lower, upper): the descent with ChildFor, lower_bound in the leaf, Take copying the keys below the bound, and Settle following the right-sibling links. The range is half open, as in the code. The count of pages read is the point: one per level to reach the first leaf, then one per leaf, with no climb back to the root.

Watch the labelling

Edit the hierarchy, then step through what \hindex build does: the spanning forest, the postorder numbers, the interval sets, and finally \under as range scans over those numbers. The set sizes obey the bound in the book: at most one plus the number of non-tree edges below the class.
